For all three platforms (**Apple App Store, Google Play, RevenueCat**), use the same subscription concept:

* **Subscription type:** Premium Monthly
* **Billing period:** 1 month
* **Auto-renewal:** Disabled (non-renewing behavior)
* **First month discount:** 50%
* **Regular monthly price:** ₦5,000
* **First month promotional price:** ₦2,500

Below are the exact details to configure.

### Renewal

Apple subscriptions are technically auto-renewable subscriptions. If you do not want auto-renewal:

After purchase, your app should direct users to manage/cancel their subscription. Apple does not provide a "monthly subscription that expires automatically" product type for this use case.

One important implementation note: Apple and Google subscriptions are designed as **auto-renewing** products. You can make your app experience behave like "one month access only" by guiding users to cancel/manage after purchase, but the store product itself remains an auto-renewable subscription.
